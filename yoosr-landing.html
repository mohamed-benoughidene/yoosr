Analytics

Know what's working. Fix what isn't.

Analytics built into every layer of the product — not bolted on after the fact.

📈
Bot vs. agent volume
See exactly what percentage of conversations your bot fully resolves without human intervention — by day, week, or custom date range.
🔍
Unanswered queries
Every question your bot couldn't answer is logged and ranked by frequency. Use it to grow your knowledge base where it matters most.
⭐
CSAT scores
After each resolved conversation, customers rate the experience. Track average scores and distribution over time to spot agent and bot performance trends.
🪙
AI token usage
Know your cost per model. Token usage is logged per operation so you can optimize which AI model handles which type of conversation.
Orders & Lead Capture

Close deals from inside the conversation.

For MENA e-commerce and retail teams, conversations are where orders happen. Yoosr lets agents capture and manage orders without leaving the chat.

💬
Create orders in one click
The contact's name and phone pre-fill automatically from the conversation. Agents just add the product and notes — done.
🔄
Track status in real time
Every order moves through New → Confirmed → Cancelled. Agents update status directly from the conversation panel or the Orders dashboard.
📤
Export & import in bulk
Export your full order list to CSV, Excel, or JSON. Import historical orders in bulk. Filter by status, search by contact, and manage everything from one table.
