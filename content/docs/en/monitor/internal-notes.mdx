# Public messages vs internal notes

When you are in a conversation, every message you send is either **public** or **internal**. This lets your team communicate with each other inside a conversation without the visitor seeing it.

## Public messages

A public message is sent to the visitor. They see it in the chat widget on your website, or in their WhatsApp/Telegram/Messenger app depending on the channel.

Public messages appear in the chat thread with your name and avatar, styled as a regular agent message.

## Internal notes

An internal note is visible only to agents in your Yoosr dashboard. The visitor never sees it. Use internal notes to:

- Leave context for a teammate who will take over the conversation
- Flag something for your team manager to review
- Document what you tried before escalating
- Coordinate with another department without the visitor seeing

Internal notes appear in the chat thread with a **yellow background** to make them visually distinct from public messages. They are labelled **Internal note** so there is no ambiguity.

## How to switch between modes

In the chat reply area at the bottom of the middle panel, you will see a tab or toggle labelled **Public** and **Internal**. Click **Internal** before typing to write a note. Click **Public** to switch back to a regular message.

The mode you selected stays active until you change it — if you switch to Internal and send several notes in a row, you stay in Internal mode. Always check which mode is active before sending.

<Callout variant="warning" title="Double-check before sending">
There is no recall or delete for sent messages. Always confirm you are in the right mode before clicking Send. A public message sent by mistake is visible to the visitor immediately.
</Callout>

## Internal notes and canned responses

You can use canned responses in internal mode, but the `/` shortcut only triggers canned responses when you are in Public mode. If you switch to Internal after triggering a canned response, the content carries over — the mode change only affects who sees the final sent message.

## What the visitor sees

From the visitor's perspective, internal notes do not exist. If you send three public messages and two internal notes, the visitor's chat window shows only the three public messages in order.
