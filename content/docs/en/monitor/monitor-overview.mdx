# The Monitor

The Monitor is your team's primary workspace. It shows every open conversation in your project in real time and lets agents read, reply, assign, and resolve — all without leaving the page.

## The three-panel layout

On desktop, the Monitor is split into three resizable panels side by side. You can drag the dividers between panels to give more space to whichever panel you need.

**Left panel — Conversation list**
All open conversations for your project, newest first. Each row shows the visitor's name, the last message snippet, how long ago it arrived, which channel it came from, and whether it is assigned or waiting. Clicking a row opens that conversation in the middle panel.

**Middle panel — Chat display**
The full message thread for the selected conversation. This is where you read what the visitor wrote and type your reply. The panel also contains the send button, the public/internal toggle, the join button, and the action menu.

**Right panel — Contact info**
Details about the visitor in the selected conversation — their name, email, phone, notes, labels, assigned agent, department, and conversation history. You can edit these fields directly in this panel.

## On mobile

On smaller screens the three panels stack into a single view. You navigate between them with back buttons — tap a conversation to see the chat, tap the contact icon to see their info, tap back to return to the list.

## What updates in real time

Everything in the Monitor is live. When a new message arrives, it appears in the chat display immediately. When a new conversation starts, it appears at the top of the list. When an agent resolves a conversation, it disappears from the list. You do not need to refresh.
