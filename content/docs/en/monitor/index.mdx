# Monitor

The Monitor is your team's primary workspace. It shows every open conversation in your project in real time and lets agents read, reply, assign, and resolve — all without leaving the page.

## What you can do

- **View all open conversations** in a live, updating list
- **Read and reply** to messages in the chat panel
- **Assign conversations** to specific agents or departments
- **Apply labels** to categorize conversations
- **Add internal notes** visible only to your team
- **Resolve conversations** when the issue is handled
- **Filter and search** to find specific conversations

## The three-panel layout

The Monitor uses a three-panel layout on desktop: conversation list, chat display, and contact info. On mobile, the panels stack into a single view.
