# Resolving, pending, and reopening

## Resolving a conversation

Resolving marks a conversation as finished. Use it when the visitor's issue is handled and no further action is needed.

To resolve:

1. Open the conversation in the Monitor.
2. Click **Resolve** in the chat display header, or use the **Send as resolved** option in the send button dropdown to send your final message and resolve in one step.

When a conversation is resolved:
- Its status changes to Closed.
- It disappears from the Monitor list and moves to History.
- If CSAT is configured, the visitor receives a rating prompt in the widget.
- The resolution is recorded in the activity log with your name and a timestamp.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Send as resolved saves a step">
Instead of sending your message and then clicking Resolve separately, click the arrow next to the Send button and choose **Send as resolved**. Your message sends and the conversation closes in one action.
</Callout>

## Pending

Pending is a temporary state for conversations that are waiting on something — typically waiting for the visitor to reply, or waiting on an external process to complete.

To set a conversation to pending, click the arrow next to the Send button and choose **Send as pending**. Your message sends and the conversation moves to a pending state.

Pending conversations:
- Stay in the Monitor list so your team can see them
- Are visually distinguished from active conversations
- Automatically return to Assigned status when the visitor sends a new message

Pending is not the same as Closed. The visitor is still in an active conversation — they can reply and the conversation continues.

## Sending as open

If you have accidentally resolved a conversation or changed its status and want to return it to open, use **Send as open**. This sends your message and sets the status back to Unassigned (status 100), allowing the routing engine to re-assign it if needed.

## Reopening after close

If a visitor sends a new message after their conversation was closed, Yoosr automatically reopens it as a new Unassigned conversation. It appears at the top of the Monitor list for your team to pick up.

The previous conversation history is preserved — agents can scroll up to see what was discussed before.

<Callout variant="info" title="Closed conversations expire after 90 days">
Yoosr keeps conversation data for 90 days. After that, closed conversations are permanently deleted. See [Data retention →](/docs/history/data-retention) for more.
</Callout>
