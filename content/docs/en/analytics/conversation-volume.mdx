# Conversation volume

The conversation volume report shows how many conversations your project handled over a selected date range, and how that volume is split between bot-handled and agent-handled conversations. It is the starting point for understanding support load and automation coverage.

## Finding the report

Go to **Analytics** in the left sidebar. The conversation volume chart and summary stats appear at the top of the page.

## Setting a date range

Use the **date range picker** at the top of the analytics page to filter all charts and stats to a specific period. Select a start date and an end date. All data on the page updates to reflect the selected range.

## Summary stats row

Above the chart, a row of stat cards gives totals for the selected period:

| Card | What it shows |
|------|--------------|
| Total conversations | All conversations created in the period |
| Bot handled | Conversations where a bot ran and closed or handed off the conversation |
| Agent handled | Conversations where a human agent sent at least one message |
| Open | Conversations still unresolved at the end of the period |
| Closed | Conversations resolved during the period |

## The volume chart

The bar chart shows daily conversation counts for the selected period. Each bar is split into two segments:

- **Bot** — conversations the bot handled without agent involvement
- **Agent** — conversations where a human agent participated

Hovering over a bar shows the exact counts for that day.

## What the bot vs. agent split tells you

A high bot percentage means your automation is handling a large share of incoming volume without requiring agent time. A low bot percentage means most conversations are reaching human agents — which may indicate your bot flows need improvement, or that your conversation types are complex enough to require human judgment.

Use this split alongside the CSAT report to check whether automated responses correlate with lower satisfaction scores.

## Usage quotas

Below the main stats, the page also shows usage quota progress bars for the current billing period:

- **Tokens consumed** — total LLM tokens used across all AI operations
- **Conversations count** — total conversations created against the project plan limit

These quotas reset at the start of each billing period and are independent of the date range you select for the charts.
