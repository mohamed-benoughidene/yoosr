# Activities vs the Analytics audit log

Yoosr has two sections that both show a record of things that happened in your project. They look similar at first glance — both are tables of logged events — but they serve different purposes and show different data.

## The Activities section

Found at **Dashboard → Activities**.

The Activities feed records **admin and configuration actions** — things agents do to manage the project. Creating a bot, changing a department, adding a label, updating operating hours. It is scoped to your current project and shows what your team did to configure and run Yoosr.

**Who it is for:** Agents and admins who want to understand what changed in the project and who changed it.

**What it records:** Configuration changes and conversation management actions (assign, resolve, transfer, label).

**Time range:** Always shows the most recent 90 days, newest first.

**Format:** Paginated table, 25 rows at a time, with load-more.

## The Analytics audit log

Found at **Dashboard → Analytics → Activity Log**.

The Analytics activity log is part of the broader analytics view and focuses on **operational events** — things that happened to conversations over time. It is designed for managers and admins who want to understand team performance and conversation volume across a date range.

**Who it is for:** Managers reviewing team output, admins running reports.

**What it records:** The same underlying activity log entries, but displayed in the context of analytics — alongside conversation volume, CSAT, and token usage — and filterable by date range.

**Time range:** Filtered by the date range you select in the Analytics page (default: last 30 days).

**Format:** Displayed within the Analytics dashboard alongside charts and metrics.

## Side by side

| | Activities section | Analytics audit log |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Dashboard → Activities | Dashboard → Analytics |
| Primary audience | All agents | Managers, admins |
| Context | Standalone feed | Part of analytics dashboard |
| Date filtering | None (always newest 90 days) | Yes (custom date range) |
| Alongside other data | No | Yes (charts, CSAT, tokens) |
| Pagination | Load more (25 at a time) | Load more (25 at a time) |

## Which one to use

Use **Activities** when you want to quickly check what happened recently — who resolved a conversation, who changed a department setting, or when a bot was deleted.

Use the **Analytics audit log** when you are doing a structured review — looking at what your team handled across a specific week or month, alongside the CSAT scores and conversation volume for that same period.
