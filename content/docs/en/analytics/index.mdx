# Analytics

Analytics give you visibility into how your team performs and how your bot is working. Use them to spot bottlenecks, measure improvements, and understand conversation patterns.

## Available analytics

**CSAT scores** — Customer satisfaction ratings collected after resolved conversations. See your average rating and read individual feedback.

**Token usage** — Track how many AI tokens your bot consumes. Useful for managing OpenRouter costs and setting rate limits.

**Conversation volume** — How many conversations you handle over time. Compare today vs. yesterday, and track trends.

**Activity log** — A complete history of what your team is doing — who resolved a conversation, who applied a label, who joined.

**Activities vs analytics** — Understand the difference between real-time activity feeds and aggregated analytics data.
