# The unassigned queue

The unassigned queue is not a separate page — it is a view state within Monitor. It contains every conversation with status **Unassigned** (100): conversations where no agent has been assigned yet, either because no agents were available at routing time or because the visitor arrived outside operating hours.

## Finding unassigned conversations in Monitor

In the Monitor conversation list, unassigned conversations show the **Unserved** indicator — a colored badge that signals no agent has joined or responded. You can filter to see only unassigned conversations:

1. Open **Monitor** from the left sidebar.
2. Select the **Status** filter and choose **Unassigned**.

The list narrows to show only conversations waiting for an agent.

## Claiming a conversation

To take ownership of an unassigned conversation:

**Option 1 — Assign to me**
1. Select the conversation in Monitor.
2. Open the three-dot menu (⋯) in the chat header.
3. Select **Assign to me**.

The conversation status changes to **Assigned** (200), your name appears as the assigned agent, and the conversation moves out of the unassigned queue.

**Option 2 — Join**
1. Select the conversation in Monitor.
2. Select the **Join** button in the conversation header.

Joining adds you to the participants list. If no agent is already assigned, joining also automatically assigns the conversation to you.

## Why conversations end up in the queue

A conversation lands in the unassigned queue when the routing engine runs and finds no available agent. Common causes:

- All agents are currently at capacity.
- No agents are logged in or marked as available.
- The HITL Handoff block ran during off-hours when no agents are present.
- The project has no active bot and no agents were available when the visitor started the conversation.

## Keeping the queue clear

An unassigned conversation means a visitor is waiting with no one actively helping them. Monitor the **Unserved** count on the Monitor page and aim to claim conversations quickly during active hours. For after-hours coverage, connect your bot flows to the **If operating hours** block so visitors know when to expect a response rather than waiting silently.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Use the Unserved filter during busy periods">
During high-volume periods, filter Monitor by Unserved to see exactly which visitors have not yet been helped. This prevents conversations from being missed when the list is long.
</Callout>
