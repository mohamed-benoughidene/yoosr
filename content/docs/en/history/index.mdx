# History

The History section is a searchable archive of every conversation that has been resolved in your project. Once a conversation is closed, it moves here.

## What you can do

- **Search by visitor name** or message content
- **Filter by date range** to find conversations from a specific period
- **View CSAT ratings** and feedback left by visitors
- **Export to CSV** for reporting and audits
- **Open any conversation** in read mode to review the full thread
