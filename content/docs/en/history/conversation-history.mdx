# Conversation history

The History section is a searchable archive of every conversation that has been resolved in your project. Once a conversation is closed from Monitor or Chat, it moves here.

## What you see in the history table

Each row in the history table represents one resolved conversation. The columns are:

**Visitor** — The name of the visitor, or "Anonymous Visitor" if no name was captured during the conversation.

**Rating** — The CSAT rating the visitor gave after the conversation closed, shown as stars (1–5). Click a rating to see the written feedback if the visitor left a comment. Conversations with no rating show "Not rated".

**Resolved by** — The agent who clicked Resolve, or "System" if the conversation was auto-closed by an inactivity rule.

**Closed at** — How long ago the conversation was closed, shown as a relative time (for example, "3 hours ago").

## Searching history

The search bar at the top filters by visitor name or the content of the last message. It searches as you type. Use it to find a specific visitor's conversation quickly.

## Filtering by date

Click the **date range picker** to filter history to a specific period. Select a start date and an end date on the calendar — the table updates to show only conversations closed within that range. Click **Clear date** to remove the filter.

## Loading more conversations

History loads 50 conversations at a time. If there are more, a **Load more** button appears at the bottom of the table. Click it to load the next 50.

## Opening a conversation

Click **View** on any row to open that conversation in the Chat section. You can read the full message thread, see the internal notes, and check what was discussed — even after the conversation is closed.

<Callout variant="info" title="You cannot reopen from History">
Clicking View opens the conversation in read mode. To reopen a closed conversation and reply to the visitor again, the visitor needs to send a new message — Yoosr automatically creates a new conversation when that happens.
</Callout>

## Exporting to CSV

Click the **Export CSV** button to download the current filtered view as a spreadsheet. The export includes the conversation ID, visitor name, last message, status code, and close timestamp. Use it for reporting, audits, or importing into another tool.
