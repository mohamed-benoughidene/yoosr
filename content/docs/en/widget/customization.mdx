# Widget Customization

Make your widget match your brand. Yoosr gives you full control over colors, logos, and positioning — all configurable from the dashboard with a live preview.

## Color Themes

Yoosr ships with five pre-built color presets:

| Preset | Color | Use Case |
|--------|-------|----------|
| **Ocean Blue** | `#2563eb` | Professional, tech-focused brands |
| **Forest Green** | `#16a34a` | Nature, health, wellness brands |
| **Royal Purple** | `#7c3aed` | Creative, premium brands |
| **Midnight** | `#0f172a` | Dark mode, modern interfaces |
| **Custom** | `#000000` | Your own brand color |

Click any preset to apply it instantly. The preview updates in real-time.

## Primary Color

For full control, use the **color picker** or **hex input**:

1. Click the color swatch to open the color picker
2. Select any color, or type a hex value (e.g., `#6366f1`)
3. The color applies to the launcher button, chat header, and interactive elements

<Callout variant="tip" title="Brand consistency">
Use your brand's primary color for a seamless experience. The widget will automatically feel like part of your site.
</Callout>

## Logo & Branding

Upload your company logo to appear in the widget header:

| Setting | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Logo URL** | A public URL to your logo image (PNG, SVG, or WebP) | `https://example.com/logo.png` |

<Callout variant="info" title="Image requirements">
Use a square image (at least 64×64px) with a transparent or solid background. SVG files scale best.
</Callout>

## Alignment

Choose where the widget appears on your page:

- **Right** (default) — Bottom-right corner, standard placement
- **Left** — Bottom-left corner, useful if you have other right-aligned widgets

The alignment setting affects both the launcher button and the chat window.

## Live Preview

The right side of the Widget Setup page shows a **live preview** of your widget inside an iPhone-style frame. The preview:

- Reflects your current color, logo, and alignment settings
- Uses your project's actual widget configuration
- Updates when you click **Save Config**

<Callout variant="warning" title="Preview limitations">
The preview runs in an iframe and may have session state. Use the "Reset Session" button below the preview to clear the visitor ID and test as a new user.
</Callout>

## Reset Session

When testing, the widget remembers your visitor session. To test as a new visitor:

1. Click the **Reset Session** button below the preview
2. This clears `yoosr_visitor_id` from localStorage
3. The widget reloads as a fresh visitor

Alternatively, open your site in an **incognito/private window**.

## Saving Changes

After making changes:

1. Click **Save Config** at the bottom of the page
2. A success toast confirms the save
3. The preview reloads to reflect changes
4. Changes propagate to all live widget instances immediately

<Callout variant="success" title="Instant propagation">
Widget configuration is fetched at runtime, not cached. Changes take effect immediately — no redeployment needed.
</Callout>
