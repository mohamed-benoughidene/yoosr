# Searching and filtering contacts

The Contacts section is a directory of all visitor profiles saved in your project. It is separate from conversations — a contact record persists even after the conversation that created it has expired.

## The contacts table

Go to **Dashboard → Contacts** to see the full list. The table shows:

- **Name** — The visitor's name
- **Email** — Their email address (sortable)
- **Phone** — Their phone number
- **Tags** — Any tags applied to this contact
- **Created** — When the contact record was created

Click a column header to sort by that column. Click the same header again to reverse the sort order.

## Searching

The search bar at the top of the table filters by name in real time as you type. It is a client-side filter — all contacts load first, then the search narrows them down.

<Callout variant="info" title="Contacts load up to 500 at a time">
The contacts list loads a maximum of 500 records. If your project has more than 500 contacts, use the export feature to access the full list.
</Callout>

## Adding a contact manually

Click **New contact** to open the create dialog. Fill in the fields:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | Display name for the contact |
| Email | No | Must be unique within the project |
| Phone | No | Must be unique within the project |
| Address | No | Free text |
| Note | No | Internal note about this contact |

Email and phone are deduplicated — if you try to create a contact with an email or phone that already exists in the project, Yoosr will reject it with an error.

## Editing a contact

Click the three-dot menu (⋮) on any row and select **Edit** to open the edit dialog. All fields are editable. The same deduplication rules apply when editing.

## Deleting a contact

Click the three-dot menu (⋮) on any row and select **Delete**. Yoosr will ask for confirmation before deleting.

You cannot delete a contact that is linked to an active (open) conversation. Close the conversation first, then delete the contact.

## Importing contacts

Click **Import** to upload a contact list in bulk. Supported formats are **CSV** and **Excel (.xlsx)**.

Your file must have a header row with at least a `Name` column. Optional columns: `Email`, `Phone`, `Address`, `Note`, `Tags`.

For CSV files, tags should be semicolon-separated in a single cell — for example: `billing;vip;arabic`.

After uploading, Yoosr shows you a preview of how many contacts will be imported and how many rows were skipped (due to missing name, duplicate email, or duplicate phone). Confirm to complete the import.

The batch import accepts between 1 and 500 contacts per file.

## Exporting contacts

Click **Export** to download your contacts in one of three formats:

**CSV** — A plain text spreadsheet. Works in Excel, Google Sheets, and any data tool.

**JSON** — Structured data format. Useful for importing into other systems programmatically.

**Excel (.xlsx)** — A formatted spreadsheet with proper column widths. Best for sharing with non-technical teammates.

The export includes all contacts currently visible in the table — if you have a search or filter active, only the filtered results are exported.
