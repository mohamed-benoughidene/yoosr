# Contacts

The Contacts section is a directory of all visitor profiles saved in your project. A contact record persists even after the conversation that created it has expired.

## What you can do

- **Search and sort** by name, email, and phone
- **Import contacts** from CSV or Excel files (up to 500 per batch)
- **Export contacts** in CSV, JSON, or Excel format
- **Edit contact details** — name, email, phone, address, and notes
- **View the contact profile** — see all conversations, labels, and attributes for a visitor
