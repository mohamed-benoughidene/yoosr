# Messenger

Connect your Facebook Page to Yoosr so Messenger conversations appear alongside all your other channels in the agent dashboard.

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

- A Meta Developer account at [developers.facebook.com](https://developers.facebook.com)
- A Facebook Page you administer
- The page linked to your Meta Developer app

## Step 1: Create a Meta app

1. Go to [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com) and sign in.
2. Select **My Apps** → **Create App**.
3. Choose **Business** as the app type.
4. Add the **Messenger** product to your app.

## Step 2: Connect your Facebook Page and get credentials

1. In your Meta App, go to **Messenger → Settings**.
2. Under **Access Tokens**, select your Facebook Page.
3. Grant the required permissions: `pages_messaging` and `pages_manage_metadata`.
4. Copy the **Page Access Token** and note your **Page ID** (visible in the Page settings on Facebook).
5. Find your **App Secret** under **App Settings → Basic** in your Meta App.

## Step 3: Configure in Yoosr

1. In the Yoosr dashboard, go to **Settings → Channels**.
2. Find **Messenger** and select **Connect**.
3. Enter your **Page ID**, **Page Access Token**, and **App Secret**.
4. Yoosr generates a **Verify Token** automatically.
5. Select **Save**.

## Step 4: Set the webhook in Meta

1. In your Meta App, go to **Messenger → Settings** → **Webhooks** → **Add Callback URL**.
2. Paste the **Callback URL** from Yoosr — it is the same endpoint used for WhatsApp: `https://<your-convex-site>.convex.site/webhooks/meta`.
3. Paste the **Verify Token** from Yoosr.
4. Select **Verify and Save**.
5. Subscribe to: `messages`, `messaging_postbacks`, `message_deliveries`, `message_reads`.

## Step 5: Test the connection

Go to your Facebook Page and select **Message**. Send a test message. A conversation appears in Yoosr with the Messenger channel indicator. Reply from Yoosr — your reply appears in the Messenger thread.

## How Yoosr tells Messenger and WhatsApp apart

Messenger and WhatsApp share the same webhook endpoint (`/webhooks/meta`). Yoosr reads the `object` field in the webhook payload to identify the source:

| `object` value | Channel |
|---------------|---------|
| `page` | Messenger |
| `whatsapp_business_account` | WhatsApp |
| `instagram` | Instagram |

## Security

Yoosr verifies every incoming webhook request using HMAC-SHA256 signature verification against the `X-Hub-Signature-256` header, using your stored App Secret. The App Secret is encrypted with AES-GCM before being written to the database. Yoosr always responds with `200 OK` to prevent Meta from retrying requests.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Messages not arriving | Check that the webhook subscription includes the `messages` event |
| `403 Forbidden` | App secret or access token mismatch — re-enter your credentials in Yoosr |
| Page not appearing in token list | Ensure you have admin access to the Facebook Page and it is linked to the Meta App |
