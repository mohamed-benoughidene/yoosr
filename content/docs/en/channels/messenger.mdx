# Messenger Integration

Connect your Facebook Page to Yoosr so Messenger conversations appear alongside all your other channels.

## Step 1: Create a Meta App

1. Go to [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com)
2. Click **My Apps** → **Create App**
3. Select **Business** as the app type
4. Add the **Messenger** product to your app

## Step 2: Connect Your Facebook Page

1. In your Meta App, go to **Messenger → Settings**
2. Under **Access Tokens**, select your Facebook Page
3. Grant the required permissions:
   - `pages_messaging`
   - `pages_manage_metadata`
4. Copy the **Page Access Token**

## Step 3: Configure in Yoosr

1. Go to **Settings → Channels** → **Messenger** → **Connect**
2. Enter your **Page ID**
3. Enter your **Page Access Token**
4. Enter your **App Secret** (from Meta App → Settings → Basic)
5. Yoosr generates a **Verify Token**
6. Click **Save**

## Step 4: Configure the Webhook

In your Meta App:

1. Go to **Messenger → Settings** → **Webhooks**
2. Click **Add Callback URL**
3. Paste the **Callback URL** from Yoosr (same as WhatsApp: `https://<your-convex-site>.convex.site/webhooks/meta`)
4. Paste the **Verify Token** from Yoosr
5. Click **Verify and Save**
6. Subscribe to: `messages`, `messaging_postbacks`, `message_deliveries`, `message_reads`

## Step 5: Test

1. Go to your Facebook Page and click **Message**
2. Send a test message
3. A conversation appears in Yoosr with the Messenger channel indicator
4. Reply from Yoosr — it appears in Messenger

## How It Works

Messenger and WhatsApp share the same webhook endpoint (`/webhooks/meta`). Yoosr distinguishes them by the `object` field in the webhook payload:

- `page` → Messenger
- `whatsapp_business_account` → WhatsApp
- `instagram` → Instagram

## Security

Same HMAC-SHA256 signature verification as WhatsApp. The app secret is used to verify the `X-Hub-Signature-256` header on every request.
