# Channels

Channels are the different ways visitors can reach your team through Yoosr. Every conversation from every channel appears in the same Monitor, so your team works in one place regardless of where messages come from.

## Available channels

**Website widget** — A chat button embedded on your website. Visitors click it and chat in real time.

**WhatsApp** — Connect your WhatsApp Business number. Messages from WhatsApp appear alongside widget conversations.

**Telegram** — Link a Telegram bot to receive and respond to messages from Telegram users.

**Facebook Messenger** — Connect a Facebook Page to handle Messenger conversations.

**Instagram** — Link your Instagram business account for DM conversations.

## How channels work

Each channel has its own setup process, but they all behave the same way once connected:

- Conversations appear in the Monitor with a channel icon
- The same bot can handle visitors from any channel
- The same agents can reply to any channel
- Routing, labels, departments, and analytics work identically across channels
