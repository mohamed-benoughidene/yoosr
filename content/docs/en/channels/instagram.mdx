# Instagram Integration

Connect Instagram DMs to Yoosr so direct messages and mentions appear in your agent dashboard.

## Prerequisites

- An **Instagram Business** or **Creator** account
- The Instagram account must be **linked to a Facebook Page**
- A **Meta Developer** app with Messenger products

## Step 1: Link Instagram to Facebook Page

1. Open Instagram → **Settings** → **Account**
2. Tap **Linked Accounts** → **Facebook**
3. Select your Facebook Page

<Callout variant="warning" title="Business or Creator only">
Personal Instagram accounts don't support the Messaging API. Convert to a Business account in Instagram Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account.
</Callout>

## Step 2: Configure Your Meta App

1. Go to [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com)
2. Open your app (or create one with **Business** type)
3. Add **Messenger** product
4. Under **Messenger → Settings**, grant Instagram permissions:
   - `instagram_manage_messages`
   - `instagram_basic`

## Step 3: Configure in Yoosr

1. Go to **Settings → Channels** → **Instagram** → **Connect**
2. Enter your **Instagram Account ID** (found in Meta App under Instagram settings)
3. Enter your **Page Access Token** (same as Messenger)
4. Enter your **App Secret**
5. Yoosr generates a **Verify Token**
6. Click **Save**

## Step 4: Configure the Webhook

In your Meta App:

1. Go to **Messenger → Settings** → **Webhooks**
2. Use the **same Callback URL** as WhatsApp/Messenger
3. Paste the **Verify Token** from Yoosr
4. Subscribe to: `instagram`, `instagram_direct`, `instagram_message_metadata`

## Step 5: Test

1. Send a DM to your Instagram account from another account
2. The conversation appears in Yoosr with the Instagram indicator
3. Reply from Yoosr — the reply appears in Instagram DMs

## What's Supported

| Feature | Supported |
|---------|-----------|
| Direct messages | ✅ |
| Image messages | ✅ |
| Quick replies | ✅ |
| Story mentions | Partial — depends on API version |
| Voice messages | ✅ |

## How It Works

Like Messenger and WhatsApp, Instagram messages arrive at the shared `/webhooks/meta` endpoint. Yoosr identifies them by the `object` field value of `instagram`.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Messages not arriving | Verify Instagram is linked to the correct Facebook Page |
| `403 Forbidden` | App secret or access token mismatch — re-enter credentials |
| Can't see Instagram option | Ensure `instagram_manage_messages` permission is granted and approved by Meta |
