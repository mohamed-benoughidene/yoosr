# Instagram

Connect your Instagram account to Yoosr so direct messages appear in your agent dashboard alongside all other channels.

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

- An **Instagram Business** or **Creator** account (personal accounts are not supported)
- The Instagram account linked to a **Facebook Page** you administer
- A Meta Developer account with a Business-type app that has the **Messenger** product added

<Callout variant="warning" title="Personal accounts are not supported">
The Instagram Messaging API requires a Business or Creator account. To convert, go to Instagram Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account.
</Callout>

## Step 1: Link Instagram to your Facebook Page

1. Open Instagram and go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Select **Linked Accounts → Facebook**.
3. Choose the Facebook Page you want to connect.

The Instagram account must be linked to the same Page you use in your Meta Developer app.

## Step 2: Configure your Meta app

1. Go to [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com) and open your existing Business app (or create one).
2. Add the **Messenger** product if it is not already added.
3. Under **Messenger → Settings**, grant these Instagram permissions: `instagram_manage_messages` and `instagram_basic`.

## Step 3: Configure in Yoosr

1. In the Yoosr dashboard, go to **Settings → Channels**.
2. Find **Instagram** and select **Connect**.
3. Enter your **Instagram Account ID** (found in your Meta App under Instagram settings).
4. Enter your **Page Access Token** (the same token you use for Messenger).
5. Enter your **App Secret**.
6. Yoosr generates a **Verify Token** automatically.
7. Select **Save**.

## Step 4: Set the webhook in Meta

Instagram uses the same webhook endpoint as Messenger and WhatsApp.

1. In your Meta App, go to **Messenger → Settings** → **Webhooks**.
2. Use the same **Callback URL** as your Messenger or WhatsApp setup: `https://<your-convex-site>.convex.site/webhooks/meta`.
3. Paste the **Verify Token** from Yoosr.
4. Subscribe to: `instagram`, `instagram_direct`, `instagram_message_metadata`.

## Step 5: Test the connection

Send a DM to your Instagram account from a separate account. Within seconds, the conversation appears in Yoosr with the Instagram channel indicator. Reply from the dashboard — your reply appears in the Instagram DM thread.

## What is supported

| Feature | Supported |
|---------|-----------|
| Direct messages | ✅ |
| Image messages | ✅ |
| Quick replies | ✅ |
| Voice messages | ✅ |
| Story mentions | Partial — depends on API version |

## How it works

Instagram messages arrive at the shared `/webhooks/meta` endpoint. Yoosr identifies them by the `object` field value of `instagram` in the webhook payload. The rest of the processing — contact matching, conversation creation, routing — is identical to Messenger and WhatsApp.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Messages not arriving | Verify Instagram is linked to the correct Facebook Page |
| `403 Forbidden` | App secret or access token mismatch — re-enter your credentials in Yoosr |
| Instagram option not available | Ensure `instagram_manage_messages` permission has been granted and approved by Meta |
