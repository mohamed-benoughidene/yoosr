# Knowledge base

A knowledge base is how your bot answers questions without hardcoded logic. You upload your documents — help articles, FAQs, product guides — and the bot searches them automatically when a visitor asks something.

## How it works

1. **Create a knowledge base** — Give it a name and description
2. **Add sources** — Upload files, paste text, or provide URLs
3. **Processing** — Yoosr splits your content into chunks and creates vector embeddings for semantic search
4. **Connect to a bot** — Add an Ask Knowledge Base block to your bot flow
5. **The bot searches** — When a visitor asks a question, the bot finds the most relevant content and uses it to generate an answer

## What makes a good knowledge base

- **Clear, well-structured content** — The bot reads your content the same way a human would. Use headings, paragraphs, and lists.
- **Focused articles** — Each source should cover one topic. Long, mixed-topic documents can confuse the search.
- **Up-to-date information** — Remove outdated articles. The bot will use whatever is currently in your knowledge base.
