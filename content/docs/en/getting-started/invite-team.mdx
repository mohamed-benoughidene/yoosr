# Invite your team

You invite teammates through your Clerk organization, not from within the Yoosr dashboard. Once a teammate accepts the invitation and joins your organization, their account is automatically available in Yoosr.

## How to invite

1. Go to your Clerk organization management page. You can reach it from the account menu in the top-right corner of the Yoosr dashboard — click your avatar, then **Manage organization**.
2. Click **Invite members**.
3. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite.
4. Choose their role: **Admin** or **Member**.
5. Click **Send invitation**.

The teammate receives an email. Once they accept and sign in, they appear in your project's team list.

## Roles

| Role | What they can do |
|------|-----------------|
| Admin | Full access — create and delete projects, manage webhook subscriptions, invite teammates, configure settings |
| Member | Handle conversations, send messages, use the agent dashboard — cannot change project settings or create webhooks |

<Callout variant="info" title="Role is set at the organization level">
Roles are managed in Clerk, not in Yoosr. If you need to change a teammate's role after invitation, do it from the Clerk organization management page.
</Callout>

## What happens after accepting

Once a teammate accepts and signs in:

- They appear in the **Routing** section as an available agent
- Their availability status defaults to **available**
- They can be assigned to conversations manually or by the routing engine
- Admins can assign them to departments

## Removing a teammate

Remove a teammate from the Clerk organization management page. Removing them revokes their access to all Yoosr projects in that organization immediately.
