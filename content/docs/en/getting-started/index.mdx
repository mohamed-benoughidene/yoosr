# Getting started with Yoosr

Yoosr is a customer support platform. Your team uses it to talk with customers in real time, build bots that answer common questions automatically, and keep every conversation — whether it comes from your website, WhatsApp, Telegram, or Messenger — in one dashboard.

## What you can do

**Live chat widget** — Paste two lines of code on any website and a chat button appears. Customers click it, you respond.

**Bot flows** — Build automated conversation flows with a visual drag-and-drop editor. No code required. The bot handles repetitive questions so your team focuses on complex ones.

**Knowledge base** — Upload your docs, FAQs, and help articles. The bot searches them automatically when a customer asks a question.

**Multiple channels** — Connect WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook Messenger, and Instagram. Every message lands in the same inbox.

**Team management** — Invite teammates, create departments, assign conversations, and track who handled what.

## How it's structured

Before you click anything, it helps to understand three terms you'll see everywhere:

**Organization** — Your company's workspace on Yoosr. Everything your team does lives inside it. When you invite a teammate, they join your organization.

**Project** — A project is the configuration for one website or brand. It holds your widget settings, bot, knowledge base, and channel connections. One organization can have multiple projects — one per website, for example.

**Bot** — A bot is an automated responder assigned to a project. It runs before a human agent joins a conversation.

The relationship is simple: your organization contains projects, and each project can have one bot.
