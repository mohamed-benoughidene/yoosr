# The dashboard home page

The dashboard home page is your live snapshot of the project. Every number on it updates in real time — you do not need to refresh.

## The four stat cards

At the top of the page you will see four cards:

**Open conversations** — The total number of conversations that are currently open across all agents and channels. This includes both assigned and unassigned ones.

**Waiting for agent** — Conversations where no agent has joined yet. These are the ones that need attention first.

**Online teammates** — How many agents in your organization are currently marked as available. This number directly affects routing — if it drops to zero, new conversations go to the unassigned queue.

**My assigned** — Conversations that are assigned specifically to you. This is your personal workload count.

## The live queue

Below the stat cards, the **Live queue** table shows every conversation that is currently open, who it is assigned to, how long the visitor has been waiting, and the conversation status.

Clicking any row in the live queue takes you directly to that conversation in the Chat section.

When the queue is empty, the table shows a confirmation message — all current conversations are handled.

## The activity feed

To the right of the live queue is the **Recent activity** feed. It shows a live log of what your team is doing — who resolved a conversation, who joined one, who applied a label. Each entry shows the agent's name, what they did, and how long ago.

Clicking an activity entry that involves a conversation takes you to that conversation directly.

If the feed is long, click **Load more** at the bottom to see earlier entries.

## Today's snapshot

At the bottom of the page, the **Today's snapshot** section shows three numbers for the current day:

**Conversations today** — How many new conversations started today compared to yesterday. A green number means more than yesterday, red means fewer.

**Bot resolved** — How many conversations the bot closed without a human agent stepping in. A higher number here means your bot is working well.

**Avg. response time** — The average time between a visitor's first message and an agent's first reply today.

These numbers reset at midnight each day.

## The bot setup banner

If your project has no bot configured yet, a blue banner appears at the top of the page with a prompt to create one. Once you create your first bot, the banner disappears permanently.
