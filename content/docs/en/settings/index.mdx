# Settings

Project settings control how your bot and team operate. These are configured from the dashboard and affect how conversations are handled around the clock.

## What you can configure

**Operating hours** — Set when your team is available. Outside operating hours, the bot can handle conversations or queue them for the next business day.

**Notifications** — Configure how and when your team gets alerts about new conversations, mentions, and handoffs.

**AI model selection** — Choose which LLM model your bot uses for answering questions and generating responses.

**Bot troubleshooting** — Diagnose why a bot is not responding or behaving as expected.
